## Premise
A Colonial Marshal task force goes to Sutter's World, on the edge of known space, to serve a criminal warrant on a dangerous cult leader. They face armed extremists, a missing corporate research vessel, an unstable lethal pathogen and hostile alien life. The marshals have to work out how these connect, take their man and survive a brutal last stand.
## Couch-night fit
5/5. Built as a 3–5 player convention one-shot with pregens, agendas, pacing notes and a finale, and the warrant-serving premise gives an AI GM a clear spine.
