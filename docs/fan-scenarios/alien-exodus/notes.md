## Premise
A colony spine-ship carrying eight drop ships to Gamma Leporis 3A secretly had a consignment of xenomorph eggs in one of them. Two weeks after the outbreak, the PCs are the last survivors, barricaded in a kitchen with improvised weapons. Then power returns and a Marine scout ship docks, apparently a rescue. Listening in on the Marines' frequency reveals their orders: recover the eggs, kill the creatures and leave no witnesses. The survivors must slip past both Marines and aliens to hide among the sleeping colonists in another drop ship, escape by pod, or turn the two threats on each other.
## Couch-night fit
4/5. A sharp, simple premise (survivors versus aliens and corporate Marines on one ship) with ready pregens and clear escape goals; it is written for 6 and has light stats.
