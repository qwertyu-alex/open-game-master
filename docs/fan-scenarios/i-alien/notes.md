- Author: Dave Semark

- Download: saved as i-alien-dave-semark.pdf
## Premise
The players are the xenomorphs. They hatch from the eggs in the derelict on LV-426 just as the Hadley's Hope colonists (158 men, women and children) find it. They go from facehugger to chestburster to adult, sharing a hive mind. Their goal is to infiltrate the colony, survive, spread through "ovomorphing" hosts, and ultimately raise a new Queen while the colony grows more alert and armed with each phase.
## Couch-night fit
2/5. A fun inversion, but there are no human pregens or agendas, it relies on improvisation and random tables, and it does not deliver the usual horror-as-victim night.
