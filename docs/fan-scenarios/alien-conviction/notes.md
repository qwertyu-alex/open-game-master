## Premise
A newly sworn Colonial Marshal escorts three violent convicts, frozen in cryo, on an automated shuttle bound for Gateway II on the Frontier, a 45-day hop. The job should be to go to sleep and wake up a day before docking. The epigraph from a "Dr. Elena Voss" ("humanity is a limitation") hints that someone is engineering something beyond human, and the trip does not go as planned. The author calls it his last Alien fan scenario.
