## Premise
Four hours into a three-day run from Alameda Station to Torin Prime, the crew of the USCSS Grendel are playing poker when an unfamiliar klaxon sounds: a motion sensor by the secure cargo bay, under a classified Weyland-Yutani data protocol. The cause turns out to be a cheerful stowaway kid, but then one of the Marines collapses and a chestburster escapes into the vents. Meanwhile the company AI is asking for a status report on cargo the company values more than the ship or anyone aboard. From there it is a crew-politics survival game against a single xenomorph scout.
## Couch-night fit
5/5. A tight 3-hour single-ship one-shot for 3–5 players, with pregens built on conflicting secrets, a simple threat and a strong cold open. It is close to ideal for an AI GM.
