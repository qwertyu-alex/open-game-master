## Premise
The UA science ship Résilience went silent two weeks into a three-month mission and drifted off course. A volunteer rescue team is assembled on the world Chute Libre with a hefty bounty, a non-disclosure agreement and ordered priorities: find survivors, recover the research, bring back specimens, return the ship, or destroy it. Aboard, the crew were cross-breeding two alien species with a genetic accelerator; a UPP spy released the hybrids into the vents, and the hybrids turned out able to implant hosts, so a xenomorph hive and queen now grow in the reactor hangar. The deck-by-deck layout includes cryotubes with three survivors, and the team itself hides a synthetic and other secrets.
