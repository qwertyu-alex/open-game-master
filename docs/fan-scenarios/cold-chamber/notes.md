## Premise
A MU/TH/UR crash report from the freighter Calisto opens the game: a drop shuttle is down, the crash site is secured, and three unidentified life forms are aboard. The scenario turns the cinematic mode up to full action, with fighting, internal intrigue and survival, and players are told not to expect to finish with the character they started with. It leans on prepared audio handouts (alarms, countdowns, NPC messages) played through a speaker.

4 - built for exactly a one-night, sound-driven, high-casualty table, and the character hand-offs suit an AI GM; check the scenario PDF for how much of it is in English versus Danish cues.
