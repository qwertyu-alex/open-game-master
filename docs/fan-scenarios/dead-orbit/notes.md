## Premise
The players are the bored, worn-out skeleton crew of the ATOL Sphacteria, a remote refuelling and service station. Something out of the dark brings the franchise's creature aboard, and the three acts throw the crew from routine into a sealed nightmare with no way out. The creature is a modified xenomorph. It follows the graphic novel's plot: a lonely station, a damaged ship arriving, and a body-horror lab twist. The agendas mix loyalty, romance and command friction, which gives the crew built-in tension.
## Couch-night fit
4/5. A self-contained crew-on-a-station three-act with ready pregens and short personal agendas, already written for Evolved rules. The crew is six people, so with 3–5 players some of them become NPCs.
