## Premise
The Kassandra is a Seegson colony ship on its maiden voyage to Origae-6, secretly sent to beat Weyland-Yutani to the planet. Mid-voyage the main crew answered a strange signal, brought infected people back from a derelict, and died. The ship's APOLLO AI locked the vessel down and kept flying. Years later the ship crash-lands in an ocean and wakes the secondary crew, the players. They do not know that the ship is underwater, that APOLLO is working under conflicting directives, or that something survived on the sealed, flooded lower decks.
## Couch-night fit
4/5. A strong single-site premise (a sunken ship, an evasive AI, flooded decks) with clear act goals that suits an overhead map. The pregens need stats filled in, and it is written for 4–6 players.
