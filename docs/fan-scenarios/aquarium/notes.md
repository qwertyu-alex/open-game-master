## Premise
On Bracken's World, a Dutch survey corporation builds a luxury resort around a vast bioluminescent coral-like megaflora. Construction kills the glow, and a rival biotech firm, ASTROBIOGEN, hacks the coral's DNA with "changer spores" and black goo, which turns one of its symbionts deadly. During a three-night investor preview most guests die, and the CEO barricades himself in with his security detail. An ecologist leads eco-terrorists in with a nuclear bomb that, unknown to her, is a dud planted by ASTROBIOGEN, which wants samples. Head office thinks it is a terrorist attack and sends the PCs, a security team with a hostage negotiator, to rescue survivors.
