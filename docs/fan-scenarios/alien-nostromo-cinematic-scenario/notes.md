## Premise
The players become the original crew of the Nostromo. The scenario replays and expands the events of the 1979 film *Alien*, keeping its slow-burn tension while adding new scenes, alternate story beats and several possible endings. Everyone at the table knows the film, so the twists come from how the scenario departs from it.
