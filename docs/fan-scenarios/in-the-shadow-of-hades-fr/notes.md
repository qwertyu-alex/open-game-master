## Premise
A game aid describing an inhabited United Americas star system and its colony, its main factions and inhabitants, and the stakes between them, so that players can play colonists facing local problems and, possibly, deadly creatures. It includes a small subsystem of its own and is presented as ready to play, but as a sandbox for campaign play rather than a scripted scenario.
## Couch-night fit
1 - a campaign sandbox with no pregens or single-night arc; useful only as setting material for a forge.
