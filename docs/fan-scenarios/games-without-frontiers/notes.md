## Premise
In the Outer Veil, the classified Weyland-Yutani research facility Crest-Magma 231.a handed goods to an authorised company team and then went dark. A hastily assembled squad of Marines and company representatives is sent to restore communications, retake the base and secure the research data. There are no xenomorphs. The threat is a custom main antagonist, plus androids with strange programming that muddy who is on whose side. It leans on Alien-universe politics, and a briefing section helps catch players up.
## Couch-night fit
4/5. Designed for exactly a 4-hour, 3–5 player single session at one facility, with pregens. Marines plus a corporate agent is more action than helpless horror, and the antagonist is not the classic creature.
