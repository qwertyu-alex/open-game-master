## Premise
The Grendel system is a frontier region fought over by three factions. In "Bug Hunt", on Nile, a desert moon that grows much of the system's food, the PCs are hired to track down a savage animal that broke into a facility and killed senior researcher Dr. Chen Fa. Behind it is Dr. Chen's research into a strain of the 26 Draconis serum (the black goo of the official Draconis saga), which has fused with Nile's wildlife into a new hybrid aberration.
