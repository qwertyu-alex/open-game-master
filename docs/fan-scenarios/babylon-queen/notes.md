## Premise
A struggling United Americas colony, New Gain on LV-661, has been half-abandoned since a meteor shower wrecked its Weyland-Yutani atmosphere processor. With the Border Wars raging, the UA warship USS Babylon has crashed near town, and colony leaders have kept away for fear of loose nuclear weapons. Weyland-Yutani now offers to rebuild the processor in exchange for intact wreckage, so the players join the salvage team sent into the crashed warship. What they don't know is that the wreck is about to drag the whole colony into a nightmare.
## Couch-night fit
4 - five pregens with agenda cards, a single crash-site and colony setting, and a convention-length arc make it a natural one-evening night, though the GM notes ask for an experienced, improvising GM.
