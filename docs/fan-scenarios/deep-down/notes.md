## Premise
On the ocean planet Voda, an isolated underwater research station is close to collapse after a diver disappears, and the team on the station face threats from the deep. It is the Dead Sea scenario, with one main change: the original's creature is replaced by a xenomorph variant, because the adapter's players wanted to face the iconic creature. The map was also rebuilt from the adapter's own VTT assets.
## Couch-night fit
3/5. It is a good one-shot underwater station, but this version is locked into Foundry, so use ../dead-sea/ for the text and treat this one as the "xenomorph variant" idea.
