## Premise
UPP soldiers or an MSS investigator are woken in an outbound UPP colony fleet when the computer of the Zakhod, the fleet's only biodome ship, detects a biohazard and a crewman goes missing. A special action cadre with full authority goes aboard the quarantined ship. An alien infestation has got in, and losing the ship would starve the colony.

4 - a quarantined biodome ship with fleet-wide stakes is a strong one-night premise; notes form only.
