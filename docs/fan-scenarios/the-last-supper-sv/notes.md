## Premise
In 2179 the crew of the cargo hauler USCSS Admetus wake from hypersleep far from their destination. Captain Pearson goes to MU/TH/UR and finds a top-priority message from Weyland-Yutani that will upend all their lives. The scenario is an Easter-themed piece built around eggs, betrayal, resurrection, crucifixion and a last supper, so expect a corporate-order twist and a crew turning on each other.
