## Premise
The passenger/cargo ship CSCSS Santa Cruz leaves Anchorpoint Station for Alexandria with 13 crew and passengers in cryo. On arrival one traveller is missing, and the scenario becomes a locked-room mystery that turns into a fight for survival. Secretly, one passenger was paid to inject three strains of black-goo pathogen into living hosts to smuggle them past ICC inspection.

5 - one session, a large pregen pool with secrets, a whodunit that turns into body horror; a close fit.
