## Premise
An Alien scenario moved to the Middle Ages: a group of medieval characters is trapped in a castle with xenomorphs. The author stresses that the players are almost completely powerless against the creatures, and that the GM should build tension steadily without terrifying the table so much that the characters simply run.
## Couch-night fit
2 - playable and short, but a medieval castle breaks the sci-fi frame a couch night promises, so it would only suit a deliberate novelty evening.
