## Premise
Three teenagers wake up trapped on a half-depressurised spaceship that is losing oxygen, with a xenomorph aboard. It is a short introductory cinematic scenario written as an open outline, so the GM improvises the flow from the elements given. A reviewer compared its tone to Stranger Things or The Goonies crossed with Alien, and wished it had a ship map.
## Couch-night fit
3 - a tight, simple survival premise that an AI GM can improvise around, but it is thin (no map, three characters) and sized for 3 rather than 3-5 players.
