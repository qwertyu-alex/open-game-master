## Premise
Novotny Station is a run-down UPP mining station on the ocean floor of the planet Voda in the GJ-1187 system. When a worker vanishes on a routine maintenance check, the crew are sent to find out what happened. The search leads outside into the dark water and to an older, abandoned station nearby, while the station itself starts failing around them and something from the depths comes after them. Getting out means salvaging parts from the old station to fix a submarine.
## Couch-night fit
5 - an isolated undersea base, a small UPP crew, a missing-person hook and an escape clock are a classic single-evening Alien horror night, and it has maps and several actual plays to learn from.
