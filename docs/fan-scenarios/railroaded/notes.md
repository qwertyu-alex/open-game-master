## Premise
An uneasy team from Weyland-Yutani, the UA Marshals and the ICC hunts a rogue synthetic on Crossroads Station, at the ragged edge of the frontier. The hunt is only the beginning: the station's factions and a crisis escalate. The expansion adds about 20 characters, more events, a new creature and a gunboat fight.
