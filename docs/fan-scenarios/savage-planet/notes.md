## Premise
A Jingti Long survey crew wakes from cryo to stake a claim on LV-2514, an Earth-like world that Weyland-Yutani has been hiding. A raptor attack has left the planet's outpost turret on auto-target, and it shoots their ship, the USCSS Harryhausen, out of the sky. Stranded among dinosaurs, Weyland-Yutani mercenaries and an Engineer ruin full of xenomorph eggs and black goo, the crew has to find a way off-world. The document adds new careers, survival rules, gear, dinosaur stats and dinosaur-hybrid xenomorphs.
