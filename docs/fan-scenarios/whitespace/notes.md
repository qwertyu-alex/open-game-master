## Premise
During the distant UPP–United Americas war, the transport Hera drops out of FTL into "Whitespace", a folded anti-space where realities and moments overlap. Another ship, the Kibenok, is trapped there in looping, endlessly repeated versions, and shows the crew the fate that awaits them. Critters native to Whitespace, including pale "albino" xenomorph variants that always seem to know where you are, hunt them as they cross shifting biomes. To escape they must gather research from several Kibenok variants and use it to restart the drive.
