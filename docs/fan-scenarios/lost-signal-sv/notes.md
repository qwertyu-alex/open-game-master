## Premise
In 2121 the Union of Progressive People is about to start terraforming the moon Selene in the SZ Ursae Majoris system, where Station 642 has been operating for ten years. The players are a hand-picked group of UPP colonists and explorers in cryosleep, on their way to deploy atmosphere processors. While they sleep, a warning light shows that something is wrong with the station orbiting Selene. The scenario plays differently depending on which of the two pregen groups the table picks.
