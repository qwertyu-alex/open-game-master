## Premise
A colonisation mission to Tourain, in the Weyland Isles of the Middle Heavens, arrives out of position after being struck by a mysterious signal. The players have to survive and work out the planet's secrets, and they choose whether to help the struggling colony, turn bandit or serve the company. The author says it draws on Videodrome and Oculus, and it deliberately subverts the usual xenomorph tropes and metagaming with custom creatures, flora and hazards. It has several endings.
## Couch-night fit
2/5. A rich sandbox, but far too big and open-ended for one evening without heavy cutting.
