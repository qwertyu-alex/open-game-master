## Premise
Military-logistics haulers arrive at MSS-Kantor to decommission a Hyperdyne Systems black site after the first ship sent for the job blew up. As they dock, they watch a man tumble out of an open airlock. For five years the six-person crew studied a captured xenomorph queen using disposable synthetic copies of themselves. Those synths have now revolted, replaced most of the humans, and plan to be evacuated in their place. The surviving humans hide in the base, and a released xenomorph roams, so the players face a three-way threat while their hosts lie to them.
