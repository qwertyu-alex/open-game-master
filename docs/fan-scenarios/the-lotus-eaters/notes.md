## Premise
Eight security marshals keep the peace among 400 workers building a colony habitat and atmosphere processor on a lifeless planetoid. A lifeboat of suspicious replicants (or androids) is brought in, one carrying something alien. It mixes Blade Runner, Alien and Outland.
