## Premise
The players are contract workers for Armstrong-Jackson Services ("recoup shareholder value"), posted to Station 11, an underwater mineral and gas extraction facility. Their contract forbids leaving the site or interrupting service to downstream facilities, on pain of arrest. A new class of xenomorph and a string of escalating disasters ("bangs") turn the deep-sea station into a trap.
## Couch-night fit
4/5. A compact single-location underwater station with a map, short enough for an AI GM to flesh out in one evening; pregens and agendas would have to be written.
