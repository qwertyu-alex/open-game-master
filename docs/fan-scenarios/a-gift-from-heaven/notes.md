- Players / length / format: three-act cinematic for 4-6 players, about 3 sessions; uses the core-book space-station map; custom "Long Stress" rule
- Pregens and secret agendas: yes (three-act agendas)
- Download: saved as a-gift-from-heaven-1.0.pdf
## Premise
A slow-burn investigation at Krakow Station, a corporate research station where the PCs study an experimental AI. As they learn more about it, they start to doubt the whole project. Little body horror; tension comes from long, high-stress work.
## Couch-night fit
2 - three sessions and a deliberately slow burn; too long for one night.
