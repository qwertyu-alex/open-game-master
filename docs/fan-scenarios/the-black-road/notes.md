## Premise
A Weyland-Yutani-funded crew steals a hauler from local gangsters, bolts guns to it and drives through the Black Road, a UPP-held jungle war zone. Their goal is a compromised dig site holding artifacts of non-human origin. War drums in the canopy, something moving under the soil, and rival powers closing in make the job a one-way trip that ends in a last stand.
## Couch-night fit
3/5. A well-built one-shot, but it is a vehicle road-run across a jungle, which fits a single-site overhead map less well than a station or ship and is closer to action than horror.
